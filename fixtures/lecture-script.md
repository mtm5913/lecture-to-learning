**AI-authored synthetic script, created November 15; revised November 17, 2026.** No real teacher or classroom. Written specifically as evaluation data; not an observed lecture, recording or machine transcript. The intervals below are illustrative labels, not measured audio timestamps. No audio is included, so no audio transcript is required. Do not present these invented classroom rules as general facts.

## F01-v1-p01 — 00:00–00:25
In this fictional class, our publishing exercise has three stages: collect, review and publish. The editor collects a permitted lecture source and prepares a draft study guide. A draft is not yet available in the student publication view.

## F01-v1-p02 — 00:25–00:50
The teacher reviews the draft, including materials created from their own lecture. Each factual study-guide claim needs a reference to the lecture passage that supports it. A real reference can still point to a passage that does not support the claim, so the reviewer must compare meanings.

## F01-v1-p03 — 00:50–01:15
The reviewer may return a draft for correction. Approval applies to a specific version. Changing an approved guide returns the changed version to draft, and the earlier approval does not automatically approve the revision.

## F01-v1-p04 — 01:15–01:40
Only the teacher can approve publication in this fictional exercise. Approval is necessary before a guide appears in the student publication view. Publication is restricted to the permitted class audience, rather than the public internet.

## F01-v1-p05 — 01:40–02:05
A transcript may contain errors. A timestamp helps the reviewer find the relevant audio, but it does not guarantee that the words were transcribed correctly. Names, numbers and negations need particular attention during review.

## F01-v1-p06 — 02:05–02:30
When a requested answer is absent from the lecture, the guide should state that the lecture does not provide enough evidence. It should not fill the gap with a plausible answer. Today I am not specifying a transcription provider, a database product or a price.
